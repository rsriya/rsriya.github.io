REGIONAL NETWORK

Proton therapy centers across Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia connected through regional academic collaboration.

FAIRFAX, VIRGINIA

Inova Schar Cancer Institute

Located within the Inova Schar Cancer Institute in Fairfax, Virginia, Inova Radiation Oncology and Proton Therapy offers state-of-the-art radiation oncology and proton therapy services for all types of cancers and benign conditions.

Visit Center Website →

WASHINGTON, DC

Johns Hopkins Proton Therapy Center

Located at Sibley Memorial Hospital in Washington, DC, the Johns Hopkins Proton Therapy Center combines clinical proton therapy with academic research and education.

Visit Center Website →

WASHINGTON, DC

MedStar Georgetown Proton Therapy

The proton therapy program at MedStar Georgetown University Hospital is part of the Lombardi Comprehensive Cancer Center and provides advanced proton therapy in Washington, DC.

Visit Center Website →

BALTIMORE, MARYLAND

Maryland Proton Treatment Center

Located in downtown Baltimore, the Maryland Proton Treatment Center integrates proton therapy with the clinical and academic programs of the University of Maryland Department of Radiation Oncology.
