EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS

Advancing knowledge through exchange.

Education is an important part of the DMV Proton Therapy Collaborative. The Collaborative provides opportunities for professionals across the region to learn from one another and exchange knowledge across disciplines.

Activities are intended to support continuing education, scientific discussion, professional development, and connections among clinicians, medical physicists, dosimetrists, radiation therapists, trainees, researchers, and other members of the proton therapy community.

FEATURED PROGRAM

DMV Proton Therapy Symposium

The DMV Proton Therapy Symposium provides a forum for professionals from across the region to share scientific work, discuss clinical and technical developments, and exchange ideas in proton therapy.

The Symposium brings together expertise from multiple institutions and disciplines, creating opportunities for scientific discussion, networking, and continued professional learning.

TRAINEES & EARLY-CAREER PROFESSIONALS

Supporting the next generation.

The Collaborative provides an opportunity for trainees and early-career professionals to connect with colleagues across the DMV proton therapy community.

Future activities may include educational sessions, mentorship opportunities, trainee presentations, and other programs designed to encourage professional development and scientific exchange.

EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES

A growing library of shared knowledge.

The Collaborative will develop a collection of educational materials and resources that can support continued learning across the regional proton therapy community.

Resources may include symposium presentations, educational talks, workshops, scientific materials, and other resources contributed by participating professionals.
