ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

Advancing proton therapy through collaboration.

A regional academic organization connecting proton therapy professionals across the DMV.

OUR MISSION

Strengthening our regional proton therapy community.

The DMV Proton Therapy Collaborative is being developed as a nonprofit academic organization connecting proton therapy centers and professionals across Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia.

The organization aims to create opportunities for education, collaboration, professional exchange, and the sharing of knowledge within the regional proton therapy community.

OUR GOALS

Collaboration that advances the field.

01

Education

Support educational opportunities and professional development within proton therapy.

02

Collaboration

Strengthen communication and collaboration among regional proton therapy centers.

03

Research

Encourage academic and scientific exchange across institutions.

04

Professional Exchange

Create opportunities for professionals to share expertise and experience.

ORGANIZATION

A regional effort, built together.

The organization is being developed jointly by proton therapy centers in the DMV region.

Organizational structure, bylaws, leadership, and membership information will be added as they are finalized.
