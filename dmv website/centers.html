THE DMV PROTON COMMUNITY

Regional expertise. Shared knowledge. Stronger together.

The DMV Proton Therapy Collaborative brings together proton therapy professionals from four treatment centers across the Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia region.

By creating opportunities for education, professional exchange, and collaboration, the organization aims to strengthen connections throughout the regional proton therapy community.
