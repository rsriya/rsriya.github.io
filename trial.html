OUR MISSION

Connecting the proton therapy community in the DMV region.

The DMV Proton Therapy Collaborative brings together professionals from leading proton treatment centers across Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia. Through collaboration and shared expertise, we aim to advance education, research, and clinical practice in proton therapy.
