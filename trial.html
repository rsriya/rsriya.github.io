01 ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

Connecting expertise
across the region.

The DMV Proton Therapy Organization is a collaborative academic organization bringing together proton therapy centers in the District of Columbia, Maryland, and Virginia.

Building on the success of the DMV Proton Therapy Symposium, the organization aims to strengthen collaboration, education, research, and professional exchange within our regional proton therapy community.
